Accessible learning · Ghana · open source

A more accessible educational web for everyone.

Extablify is an offline-first browser extension designed to make digital learning more navigable for students with disabilities, starting with Ghanaian schools.

Technology shaped around real learners, educators and communities.

01 · About

Built from an inclusion-first perspective.

Extablify brings practical accessibility tools into the browser so learners can interact with educational content in ways that better match their needs.

The project is free and open source, with an offline-first approach intended to reduce barriers where connectivity, accounts or expensive assistive technology can become obstacles.

Starting point: Ghanaian educational websites and learners with vision impairments, low vision and dyslexia.

02 · Accessibility

Tools that reduce friction.

  • 01

    Text-to-speech

    Listen to page content instead of relying only on visual reading.

  • 02

    High contrast

    Increase visual distinction between content and its background.

  • 03

    Text scaling

    Make educational content easier to read without leaving the page.

  • 04

    Content cleaner

    Reduce distracting page elements and bring the important content forward.

  • 05

    Future voice controls

    Planned speech-controlled navigation and hands-free interactions for users with motor disabilities.

  • 06

    More inclusive modes

    Planned captions, screen-reader support and simpler-language presentation.

03 · Team

People behind the project.

Extablify grew from a collaboration between disability and rehabilitation practice and technology development.

The goal is not simply to add technology to education, but to make the technology itself more usable, inclusive and relevant to the people it serves.
